Step 02 — Dataset & Data Architecture

The tables are connected through primary and foreign key relationships, allowing analysis across different business areas.

Main relationships:
- Orders → Customers
- Order Items → Orders, Products and Sellers
- Payments → Orders
- Reviews → Orders
- Shipping → Orders

Business Analysis Opportunities
The dataset supports investigations into:
- Revenue and sales performance.
- Customer purchasing behavior and retention.
- Product and category performance.
- Payment methods and transaction values.
- Delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction.

 Deliverable
 8 CSV files, a README file, and a data dictionary, packaged into a single ZIP archive.
