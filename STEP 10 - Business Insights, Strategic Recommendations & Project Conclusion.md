## 01. Executive Summary

NovaCommerce is an end-to-end Data Analytics project developed to simulate a real-world e-commerce business environment.

The project followed the complete analytical workflow, starting with business understanding and data preparation, progressing through SQL analysis and dimensional modeling, and concluding with an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The final objective was not simply to visualize data, but to transform business information into meaningful insights and actionable recommendations.

### Project at a Glance

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Records | 121,100 |
| Orders Analyzed | 20,000 |
| Units Sold | 44,414 |
| Purchasing Customers | 6,046 |
| Net Order Value | 6,065,773.69 |
| Average Order Value | 303.29 |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | 72.63% |

*The dataset represents a simulated e-commerce business and its results should be interpreted within that context.*

## 02. Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several important patterns across sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, and geographical markets.

### Insight 01 — Customer Retention

**Repeat customers represent a significant portion of the purchasing customer base.**

- Repeat Customers: 4,391
- One-time Customers: 1,655
- Repeat Purchase Rate: 72.63%

**Business Interpretation**

A substantial proportion of purchasing customers placed more than one order during the analyzed period.

This suggests that customer retention is an important component of the business model.

**Opportunity**

Continue investing in customer retention strategies, loyalty initiatives, and personalized communication to encourage additional purchases.

### Insight 02 — Electronics Dominates Revenue

**Electronics generated approximately 43.85% of total net order value.**

- Electronics Revenue: 2,660,202.04
- Total Net Order Value: 6,065,773.69

**Business Interpretation**

Electronics represents the largest revenue-generating product category in the dataset.

This creates an opportunity to investigate whether the category's performance is driven by pricing, demand, product availability, or purchasing frequency.

**Opportunity**

Prioritize category-level performance monitoring and investigate opportunities to expand complementary product sales.

### Insight 03 — Geographic Concentration

**The United States represents the largest geographical market.**

- United States: 3,487,275.70
- Canada: 984,319.66
- United Kingdom: 953,798.74
- Australia: 640,379.59

**Business Interpretation**

The United States accounts for approximately 57.5% of the total net order value.

This indicates a significant concentration of business activity in one market.

**Opportunity**

Evaluate the potential for international market expansion while maintaining the performance of the existing customer base.

### Insight 04 — Sales Channel Distribution

**The Website and Mobile App are the two largest sales channels.**

| Sales Channel | Net Order Value |
|---|---:|
| Website | 2,550,781.66 |
| Mobile App | 2,313,588.92 |
| Marketplace | 1,201,403.11 |

**Business Interpretation**

The Website and Mobile App together account for the majority of recorded net order value.

The Marketplace also contributes meaningfully to the business.

**Opportunity**

Compare conversion rates, customer acquisition costs, and average order values across channels before deciding where additional investment would generate the greatest return.

### Insight 05 — Revenue Stability

**Annual net order value remained relatively stable between 2023 and 2025.**

| Year | Net Order Value |
|---|---:|
| 2023 | 2,003,129.42 |
| 2024 | 2,048,500.59 |
| 2025 | 2,014,143.68 |

**Business Interpretation**

The business maintained a relatively consistent level of recorded net order value throughout the three-year period.

However, the results do not demonstrate sustained revenue growth.

**Opportunity**

Investigate the drivers of revenue growth through customer acquisition, purchase frequency, product mix, and average order value.

## 05. Data & Analytical Limitations

A reliable analysis must acknowledge what the available data can and cannot demonstrate.

The following limitations were identified:

**1. Simulated Dataset**

The project uses a generated e-commerce dataset. Therefore, its findings demonstrate analytical methodology rather than the actual performance of a real company.

**2. Revenue Interpretation**

The reported Net Order Value includes orders across different statuses, including cancelled and processing orders.

Consequently, it should not be interpreted as confirmed realized revenue or cash received.

**3. Customer Segmentation**

Customer segments were classified using historical purchasing activity across the available 2023–2025 period.

The segmentation does not dynamically change based on a selected reporting period.

**4. Business Impact**

The recommendations have not been implemented or experimentally tested. Their expected impact remains hypothetical.

**5. Additional Business Metrics**

Metrics such as Customer Acquisition Cost, Customer Lifetime Value, and conversion rate require additional data that was not included in the current analytical model.

## 08. Final Conclusion

NovaCommerce represents the complete development of an end-to-end Data Analytics project.

Throughout the process, the focus evolved from understanding a business problem to preparing data, investigating patterns, designing an analytical model, and presenting findings through an interactive reporting solution.

The most important outcome was not a single KPI or dashboard.

It was the ability to connect different stages of the analytical process:

**Business Understanding → Data Preparation → SQL Analysis → Data Modeling → Power BI → Business Insights**

This project demonstrates practical experience with SQL, Python-based data preparation, MotherDuck, dimensional modeling, Power Query, DAX, and Power BI.

More importantly, it demonstrates an understanding that data analysis is not simply about calculating numbers.

It is about asking the right questions, understanding what the numbers mean, recognizing limitations, and helping businesses make better decisions.
